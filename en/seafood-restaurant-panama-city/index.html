El Cangrejo · Panama City

Seafood restaurant in Panama City since 1978

Siete Mares is where Panamanian families have celebrated birthdays, graduations and Sunday lunches for almost 50 years: seafood from our own Caribbean, paella made to order and live piano at night.

Seen Casco Viejo? Now eat where locals eat

After a day in the old town, come to El Cangrejo for dinner the way Panamanians do it. Siete Mares opened in 1978, and many of our guests have been coming back for decades to celebrate with family. It is a short ride from most hotels in the city.

From the Panamanian Caribbean to your table

Our centollo comes mainly from San Blas. Lobster comes from San Blas and the Caribbean coast of Colón. Both arrive at our kitchen fresh and alive.

Lobster, three ways

Not every preparation is printed on the menu, so just ask your waiter.

  • A la plancha: grilled, for the pure flavor of the lobster.
  • Thermidor: the classic gratin.
  • Al ajillo: with garlic.

Paella, made to order

We make three paellas, each priced per person with a minimum of two: Paella Mixta, Paella Cuatro Carnes (four meats) and Paella Vegana. You can add lobster to any of them. If you prefer a juicier seafood rice, ask for the Arroz Marinera. Paella to go is ready in 30 minutes.

Live piano and private rooms

From Thursday to Saturday, dinner comes with live piano. For private celebrations and business dinners we have private rooms for groups of 10 to 110 people.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Siete Mares?

In El Cangrejo, in the center of Panama City: Avenida 1a B Norte. We have nearly 100 covered parking spaces on three floors.

Which hotels are close to Siete Mares?

A few blocks away: Sortis Hotel, Renaissance Panama City, AC Hotel by Marriott, Global Hotel, Hyatt Regency, Bristol Panamá, La Residence, Hotel El Ejecutivo and W Panama. A short taxi ride away, on Avenida Balboa and the Cinta Costera: Waldorf Astoria, Hilton Panama, Le Méridien, InterContinental Miramar and JW Marriott.

Where does the seafood come from?

Our centollo comes mainly from San Blas, in the Panamanian Caribbean. Lobster comes from San Blas and the Caribbean coast of Colón. Both arrive fresh and alive.

Do I need a reservation?

We recommend it. You can book online through Riservi, on WhatsApp at +507 6621-5905 or by calling +507 264-0144.

Is there a dress code?

No. Everyone is welcome. It is a formal restaurant, and most guests dress business casual.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Yappy and cash.

Do you have vegetarian options and a kids menu?

Yes. Our menu includes vegetarian dishes, a vegan paella and a kids menu.
